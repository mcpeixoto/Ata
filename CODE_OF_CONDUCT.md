# Code of Conduct

Ata follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, welcome people who are new, and keep criticism about the
work rather than the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks have no place here, in
issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/mcpeixoto) rather than in a public thread. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, or ban contributors,
who don't follow this code.
