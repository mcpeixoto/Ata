# Ata

Ata records your meetings on iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac, and turns them into
transcripts you can search. Transcription runs on your device.

## Help

- **Something isn't working, or you have a question:** open an issue at
  <https://github.com/mcpeixoto/Ata/issues>. Say which device and version
  (Settings → About on iPhone) you are using.
- **Subscriptions:** Ata Pro is billed by Apple. Cancel or change it in the
  iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore it on a new iPhone,
  open Ata → Settings → Ata Pro → Restore Purchases.
- **iCloud sync:** turn it on in Ata's Settings on each device, signed in to
  the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on.
