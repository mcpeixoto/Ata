# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 29 October 2026_

Ata records meetings and turns them into transcripts. It is built so that
your recordings belong to you and nobody else, including us.

## What we collect

Nothing. Ata has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash
reporting service and no server of its own. We never receive your audio,
your transcripts, your notes or anything about how you use the app.

## Where your meetings live

- **On your device.** Recordings, transcripts, notes, summaries and the voice
  fingerprints Ata uses to recognise who is speaking are stored on your iPhone,
  Apple Watch or Mac.
- **In your own iCloud, if you turn on iCloud sync.** Ata then keeps the same
  files in your iCloud Drive so your devices can share them. They are stored by
  Apple under your Apple Account; we cannot read them.

You can delete any meeting from the app, and deleting the app removes what it
stored on that device.

## What leaves your device

- **Transcription and summaries run on your device** with Apple's speech
  recognition, Apple Intelligence or a local Whisper model.
- **OpenAI, only if you choose it on the Mac.** If you paste your own OpenAI API
  key into the Mac app and pick OpenAI as the engine, the audio of the meetings
  you transcribe is sent to OpenAI under your account and its terms. The key
  stays in your Mac's keychain. The iPhone app never sends anything to OpenAI.
- **Model downloads.** The first time it needs them, Ata downloads speech and
  speaker models from Hugging Face. Nothing about you is sent with the request.
- **Updates on the Mac.** The Mac app checks GitHub for new releases.

## Purchases

The optional Ata Pro subscription on iPhone is sold and managed by Apple. We
receive no payment details; Apple only tells the app whether a subscription is
active.

## Recording other people

The law on recording conversations differs between countries. Ask the people
you record for their consent first.

## Children

Ata is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
