# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

Ata records meetings and turns them into transcripts. It is built so that
your recordings belong to you and nobody else, including us.

## What we collect

One anonymous count a day, so we know how many people use Ata and on which
versions. Each device sends:

- a random id created only for this, not linked to you, your Apple Account or
  your meetings;
- whether it is the Mac or the iPhone app, the app version and build, and the
  system version;
- your language setting;
- how many meetings were recorded on it since the last count, and how many
  minutes they add up to;
- counts of what happened in the app since the last count: the furthest
  onboarding step reached, how often the Pro screen was shown and what opened
  it, a trial, a purchase, hitting the free limit, and the same kind of count
  when a locked feature was tapped. These are numbers. They do not include a
  recording, a transcript, a title or a name;
- on iPhone, the Apple Search Ads campaign id for this install, or the word
  "organic" when the install did not come from one of those ads. The id names
  the campaign, not you. Ata asks Apple once, after setup, and only if this
  count is turned on.

Our server reads the country from the connection and keeps that, not your IP
address. Turn it off in Settings with "Share anonymous usage stats"; the app
then sends nothing more and forgets the id.

In the App Store privacy label this is Usage Data, not linked to you and not
used to track you. The privacy manifest lists it as product interaction (the
funnel counts) and other usage data (the rest of the daily count, including
the campaign id), both for analytics, with linked and tracking set to false.

That is all. Ata has no accounts and no crash reporting service. The app
does not show ads; the campaign id above only says which Search Ads campaign
an install came from, when it came from one. We never receive your audio,
your transcripts, your notes, meeting titles or the names of the people in
them.

## Where your meetings live

- **On your device.** Recordings, transcripts, notes, summaries and the voice
  fingerprints Ata uses to recognise who is speaking are stored on your iPhone
  or Mac.
- **In your own iCloud, if you turn on iCloud sync.** Ata then keeps the same
  files in your iCloud Drive so your devices can share them. They are stored by
  Apple under your Apple Account; we cannot read them.

You can delete any meeting from the app. On iPhone, deleting the app removes
what it stored on the device. On the Mac, deleting the app leaves
the meetings in `~/Documents/Meetings` (and in iCloud Drive if sync is on)
until you delete that folder.

## Agents

Agent access is off by default. When you turn it on, an agent app on the same
Mac can read your meetings through Ata. Ata opens no network port and sends
nothing. The agent app may send what it reads to its own AI provider, under
that provider's policy. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.

## What leaves your device

- **Transcription and summaries run on your device** with Apple's speech
  recognition, Apple Intelligence or a local Whisper model.
- **Model downloads.** The first time it needs them, Ata downloads speech and
  speaker models from Hugging Face. Nothing about you is sent with the request.
- **Updates on the Mac.** The Mac app checks GitHub for new releases.
- **The daily count** described above, unless you turn it off.

## Purchases

The optional Ata Pro subscription on iPhone is sold and managed by Apple. We
receive no payment details; Apple only tells the app whether a subscription is
active.

## Recording other people

The law on recording conversations differs between countries. Ask the people
you record for their consent first.

## Children

Ata is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
