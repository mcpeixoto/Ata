## Review notes (replace the current ones)

> No account or sign-in is needed.
>
> Ata records audio with the iPhone microphone and transcribes it on the device (Apple SpeechAnalyzer). Audio, transcripts and voices never leave the device, except into the user's own iCloud Drive container when the optional iCloud sync is on.
>
> Anonymous usage count: once a day, only if the user agreed in onboarding or Settings, the app sends aggregate counts (a random id used only for this, app and OS version, language, meetings and minutes recorded, and how far the user got in onboarding and the paywall). Once, after onboarding and only with that consent, it asks Apple's AdServices for the Search Ads campaign id of the install (no ATT prompt; AdServices does not require one). Nothing is linked to the user. Privacy label: Product Interaction and Other Usage Data, not linked, analytics.
>
> Background modes: "audio" keeps a recording going with the screen locked; "processing" (BGContinuedProcessingTask) finishes a long transcription after the user leaves the app.
>
> Ata Pro (auto-renewable, group "Ata Pro"): ai.interactive.ata.pro.monthly and ai.interactive.ata.pro.yearly (7-day free trial). The first 300 minutes recorded on the iPhone each month are free. The paywall appears at the end of onboarding (with "Continue free"), from Settings > Ata Pro, from a locked Pro feature, and when starting a recording with no minutes left. Pro features: Ask (Meetings tab, magnifier button; needs Apple Intelligence), custom summary templates (Settings and a meeting's summary), PDF/Word export (a meeting's share menu).
>
> A Mac companion app (distributed outside the App Store) shares meetings through iCloud and reads the iPhone subscription to unlock itself. The iPhone app never offers or links to any other way to pay.
